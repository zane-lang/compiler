# Parenthesis-balance proof experiment, 2026-09-24

Grammar: lib/cst/parser.mly at c9a146e (unchanged).
Toolchain: OCaml 5.3.0, dune 3.24.1, Menhir 20260209, sedlex 3.7.

The generated automaton has 484 distinct reduction texts; every reduction
contains equal numbers of LPAREN and RPAREN terminals. The prover now verifies
that condition at runtime before carrying a bounded exact parenthesis count
in its history state. An unmatched RPAREN is pruned immediately, and an EOF
with a known positive count cannot accept. Counts above 8 become unknown and
remain unknown, so this cannot discard a concrete derivation.

Short comparison, each with AMBIGUITY_MEMORY_MB=6144,
AMBIGUITY_MAX_FRONTIER_RATIO=1, AMBIGUITY_JOBS=1,
AMBIGUITY_MENHIR=menhir, AMBIGUITY_PROGRESS_SECONDS=0:

  ambiguity_search.exe lib/cst/parser.mly --max-tokens 0 --timeout 60 \
    --max-witnesses 1 --prove 1

  With balance: NOT PROVEN (timeout); 452,548 pairs;

The 13-token historical witness from 2026-09-18 has zero derivations in the
current grammar. This experiment removes a particular source of spurious
paths, but it does not close the remaining full-grammar proof obligation in
issue #107. A larger complete run or a stronger sound abstraction is needed.
