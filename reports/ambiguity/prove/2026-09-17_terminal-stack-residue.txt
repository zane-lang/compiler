# Terminal-stack residue
#
# Engine: tools/ambiguity_search.ml built with ocamlopt 4.14.1.
# Menhir: 20260209, the repository's pinned version.
# Grammar: lib/cst/parser.mly at 9d2c5af plus the prover changes recorded here.
# Date: 2026-09-17.

The recurring MATCH/constructor candidate was not a bounded top-K problem.
Every extra nested MATCH hid one more brace below the retained suffix, so every
finite level admitted another false accepting pair. Restarting a refined walk
removed stale candidates but exposed the same recursive family one level down.

The prover now carries a 1024-class XOR residue of the terminal-labelled edges
in the whole viable stack. Shifts add one terminal fingerprint; reductions
remove the direct terminal fingerprints in their RHS. Guessed sources and goto
targets must have the resulting residue on some automaton path from state 0.
This is conservative: every concrete LR stack is such a path, and collisions
only admit extra paths.

A minimized recursive MATCH grammar that previously survived every finite
top-K level is now proven at level 1:

  PROVEN UNAMBIGUOUS (206 abstract pairs explored)

The 17-production reduction of the Zane failure is likewise proven at level 1
in 406 pairs. The soundness and precision corpus passes all 68 prover tests,
including every known-ambiguous grammar at levels 1, 2 and 3.

The full grammar no longer reports the old false candidates. With 1024 residue
classes, level 1, a 2 GiB budget and a four-minute deadline it reached 774,226
pairs with zero accepting abstract pairs before timing out. Level 2 was worse:
1,726,526 pairs in the same time with a still-growing queue. A 64-class residue
produced a large collision-born Cartesian burst; structured delimiter-only and
order-sensitive group variants were also measured and rejected.

Two exact performance changes remain with the residue:

- after parse histories diverge, each reduction chain is closed independently
  and only its final stacks are paired;
- final stack pairs are canonicalized before entering the result table.

These preserve the explored relation and remove repeated intermediate products.
The full Zane grammar is therefore still not proven within the measured local
budget. The minimized recursive abstraction failure is closed, but an unfinished
walk cannot establish that all other blind spots are closed. The immediate
obstacle is the growing finite-state queue; a later state may still reveal
another spurious candidate or a real ambiguity.
